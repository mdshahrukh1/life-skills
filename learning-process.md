# Learning Process

**1. How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique**

**Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**\
**Answer:**\
The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you explain a concept in simple words by yourself, as if teaching someone else, to help you understand it better.


**2. Learning How to Learn – Barbara Oakley**

**Question 2: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?**\
**Answer:**\
  The most interesting thing for me was Barbara Oakley's journey. She was not good at maths and science during her school days, but later she worked on improving herself and became an engineering professor.\
  This made me realise that struggling with a subject doesn't mean we cannot learn it. With the right approach, practice, and patience, we can get better at subjects that we find difficult.

**Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**\
**Answer:**\
  Focused Mode: This is when we give our full attention to something and try to understand or solve a particular problem.\
  Diffused Mode: This is when we take a break and let our mind relax. During this time, our brain can make connections and sometimes help us understand things that we were struggling with earlier.


**3. Learn Anything in 20 Hours – Josh Kaufman**

**Question 4: According to the video, what steps should you take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**\
**Answer:**
1. Break the skill into smaller parts.
2. Learn enough to self-correct.
3. Remove practice barriers.
4. Practice at least 20 hours.


**4. Learning Principles in the Bootcamp**

**Question 5: What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?**\
**Answer:**
1. I will give my full attention to the task I am working on instead of getting distracted.
2. I'm gonna keep my phone away while studying and stay focused.
3. Whenever I learn a new concept, I try to understand where it is used, why it is used, and how to implement it in code.
4. Instead of memorizing definitions, I will try explaining the topics that I understood to my colleagues and discuss them with them.
5. I will practice coding and revisit the topics I forget often.
6. I will set realistic deadlines for completing assignments and tasks assigned.
7. If I get stuck or face difficulty understanding something, I will ask my colleagues or seniors for help.
