# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

* In plain terms, sexual harassment is any unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature — it doesn’t have to be physical, and it doesn’t have to be obvious.
It can look like:

* Unwanted touching or closeness — pinching, stroking, blocking your way, standing too close.

* Asking for sexual favours — directly or indirectly, especially with pressure or hints about work consequences.

* Sexual comments or jokes — remarks about your body, appearance, or sex life.

* Other creepy or unwelcome conduct — leering, stalking, inappropriate messages, etc.

* And it’s not just men harassing women — it can happen between anyone, including people of the same gender.
  

## 2. What would you do if you faced or witnessed it?

* If it happens to me:

* Say stop if I feel safe. I don’t owe anyone a smile or an apology — a clear “That’s unwelcome, please stop” is enough.

* Writing it down. Date, time, place, what happened, who saw it. Details matter later.

* Report it to the Internal Committee (IC). Put it in writing. I would do this within 3 months of the incident.

* If I witness it:

* Interrupt safely if I can — even asking “Are you okay?” can break the moment.

* Check in with the person and offer support.

* Encourage them to report, or offer to go with them.

* I can also report it myself to the IC or a manager — I don’t have to stay silent just because it didn’t happen to me.
